What you can calculate

1

Net / Gross

Convert a salary between net, gross I and gross II (the employer's total cost), with tax and both sets of contributions.

2

Flat-rate tax (paušal)

Monthly and yearly tax and contributions for the 15 activities with the same base across Serbia, by year in business.

3

Maternity leave

Monthly maternity and childcare leave pay, with the legal minimum and maximum base.

4

Author's fee

Tax and contributions on an author's fee, with 50%, 43% or 34% standardized expenses.

5

Independence test

Check, client by client, whether you meet at least 5 of the 9 criteria, with each one explained.

6

Bookkeeping entrepreneur

Compare self-taxation with paying yourself a salary, and see which leaves you more per year.

7

Dividend

Corporate profit tax and dividend tax when an LLC (DOO) owner pays out profit.

Why Isplata

  • 2026 parameters: the non-taxable amount, minimum and maximum contribution base, and every tax and contribution rate.
  • The result is always on top, with every line item laid out clearly.
  • Nothing leaves your phone. No account, no sign-up, and the amounts you enter are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Serbian in Latin or Cyrillic, or English, in a light or dark theme.
  • Free, with banner ads.

Good to know. Isplata gives informational calculations and is not legal, tax or financial advice. For specific decisions, consult an accountant or the Tax Administration. It is not an official app of the Serbian Tax Administration or any government body, and is not affiliated with them.
