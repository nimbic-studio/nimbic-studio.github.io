Privacy Policy - Isplata

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Isplata ("the App"), a set of Serbian salary and tax calculators published by Nimbic, handles information.

No account, no personal information collected by the App

Isplata does not require you to create an account or sign in, and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information.

Your calculations stay on your device

The amounts you enter (salaries, fees, profits and similar figures) and the answers you give in the independence-test checklist are used only to calculate the result on your device. They are not saved and are not sent to Nimbic or anyone else; they are discarded when you leave the screen or close the App.

Settings

Your chosen language, script and theme are stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes them.

No analytics

The App does not include analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools of its own.

Device permissions

The App uses network access only to show ads. It does not request access to your location, camera, contacts, microphone, files or notifications.

Advertising

The App shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect your device's advertising ID and other technical data as described in Google's own policies. Where required by law (for users in the European Economic Area and the UK), the App asks for your consent before any personalized advertising is shown, through an in-app consent prompt, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

You can learn more about how Google uses this data here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the App changes. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to nimbic.studio@proton.me.
