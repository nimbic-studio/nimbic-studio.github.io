Privacy Policy - Gatehold

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Gatehold game ("the Game"), published by Nimbic, handles information. It applies to the mobile and PC versions.

No account, no personal information

Gatehold does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information to play.

Your progress

Your progress (unlocks, levels, runs in progress, settings) is saved on your device. To keep it safe if you change or lose your device, the Game also keeps a copy of your saved progress with Unity Gaming Services (Cloud Save), a service provided by Unity Technologies. This copy is linked to a random player ID created by the Game, not to your name or any account. It contains only game progress.

Anonymous play data (only if you agree)

The first time you open the Game, it asks whether you want to share anonymous play data. If you agree, the Game sends Unity Analytics (Unity Gaming Services) information about your runs, such as the champion, companions and region you chose, how far a run went and whether it was won, together with basic technical data (device model, operating system, game version, country derived from the connection). We use it only to balance and improve the Game. If you decline, no play data is sent. You can change your choice at any time in Settings ▸ Share play data.

Crash reports

If the Game crashes or hits an error, a technical report (the error, the game version, device model and operating system) may be sent to Unity Cloud Diagnostics so the problem can be fixed. Crash reports contain no personal information.

Unity

Cloud Save, Analytics and crash reports are provided by Unity Technologies, which processes this data on our behalf under its own Privacy Policy.

Advertising and purchases

The current version of the Game shows no ads and offers no purchases. If optional rewarded ads or in-app purchases are added, this policy will be updated before they are released, and any consent required by law will be asked in the Game.

Device permissions

The Game uses network access for Cloud Save, Analytics (if you agree) and crash reports. It does not request access to your location, camera, contacts, microphone or files.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the Game deletes the progress saved on your device. To have the cloud copy of your progress and any play data deleted, contact us at the address below and include the player ID shown in the Game's Settings.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the Game changes. Continued use of the Game after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to nimbic.studio@proton.me.
