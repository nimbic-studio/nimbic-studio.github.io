LEvents

Everything happening in Leskovac, in one place.

Concerts, exhibitions, theatre, sports, workshops, films and fairs. See what's on today, this weekend or in the days ahead, without digging through social media.

A look inside

What you can do

Pick a day

Events for today, any of the next 13 days, or every upcoming event at once, with featured events at the top.

Filter and search

Concerts, exhibitions, theatre, sports, workshops, films and fairs. Combine several categories, or search by name.

All the details

Date, time, venue, a description and a link for more, plus what else is happening at the same venue.

Made to be simple

  • No account, no sign-up: open the app and start browsing.
  • Save the events you don't want to miss. They're kept on your phone, and past ones stay visible, marked as finished.
  • Serbian or English, in a light or dark theme.
  • Free, with banner ads.

Events are added by hand. Organising something in Leskovac and want it in the app? Write to nimbic.studio@proton.me.

Get LEvents

LEvents is free on Google Play, for Android 7.0 and newer.
