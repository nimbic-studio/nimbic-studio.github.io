Privacy Policy - LEvents

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the LEvents mobile app ("the App"), published by Nimbic, handles information.

No account, no personal information collected by the App

LEvents does not require you to create an account or sign in, and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information to use it.

Saved events

When you save an event in the App, that is stored locally on your device only. It is never sent to, or stored on, any server tied to your identity.

Device permissions

The App requests only network access, needed to load event listings and images. It does not request access to your location, camera, contacts, or microphone.

Advertising

The App displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect device identifiers and other technical data as described in Google's own policies. Where required by law (for users in the European Economic Area and the UK), the App asks for your consent to this before any personalized advertising is shown, through an in-app consent prompt.

You can learn more about how Google uses this data here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the App changes. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to nimbic.studio@proton.me.
