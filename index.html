Our apps

LEvents

Everything happening in Leskovac, in one place.

  • Browse by day, filter by category, or search

  • In Serbian and English, with light and dark themes

Slovca

A daily word puzzle in Serbian.

  • Guess the hidden word in six tries - the same daily word for everyone
  • Keep your streak going, and catch up on missed days from the calendar
  • Endless play, 4- and 6-letter words, two words at once, and a 3-minute blitz
  • Cyrillic or Latin, colour-blind mode, light and dark themes - no sign-up

Isplata

Serbian salary and tax calculators in one app.

  • Net and gross salary, flat-rate (paušal) tax, author's fees, dividends and entrepreneur profit tax
  • Maternity leave pay, and the independence test for freelancers
  • Up to date with 2026 rates, with notes on what each calculation covers
  • Serbian (Latin and Cyrillic) and English, light and dark themes - no sign-up, nothing stored
