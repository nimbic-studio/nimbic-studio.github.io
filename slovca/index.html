Slovca

A daily word puzzle in Serbian.

One word a day, the same for everyone, and six tries to guess it. Keep your streak going, play five other ways, and learn what each word means.

How to play

Type a five-letter word and the colours show how close you are.

K

Green: the letter is in the right spot.

R

Yellow: the letter is in the word, but in another spot.

T

Grey: the letter isn't in the word.

PATKA
KARTA

Six ways to play

Daily word

One word a day, the same for everyone. Compare results with friends.

Endless

A new word whenever you like.

4 letters and 6 letters

Shorter and longer words, for a change of pace.

Duo

Two words at once, with one set of guesses.

Blitz

How many words can you guess in three minutes?

Hard mode

For experienced players: every hint you've found must be used in your next guesses.

A look inside

Keep your streak, play your way

  • Streaks with shields: every seven days you earn a shield that saves your streak when you miss a day. The calendar shows every day you've played, and you can catch up on missed words.
  • Learn something new: after every game you see what the word means.
  • Latin or Cyrillic: the keyboard and the board follow your choice.
  • Light or dark theme, and a colour-blind mode.
  • No sign-up, no account. Your progress stays on your phone. Sign in to Google Play Games if you'd like achievements and leaderboards.
  • Free. Optional rewarded ads, for example for a hint or to play a missed day.

Found an odd word? Report it from the game: the word list keeps growing. The game itself is in Serbian.
